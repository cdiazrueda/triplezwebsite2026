Did you know?

More than a great place to hire

The second most biodiverse country in the world

From rainforests to coastlines, Colombia's ecosystems are home to more bird species than any other country.

A top coffee producer

Colombia is the world's third-largest coffee producer, and its Coffee Triangle is a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Cultural heritage

Colombia is the birthplace of celebrated authors, artists and musicians, including Gabriel García Márquez, whose One Hundred Years of Solitude popularized magical realism.

The land of emeralds

Colombia is the world's leading producer of emeralds, with a mining tradition going back more than 500 years.

A booming tech scene

Bogotá and Medellín are fast-growing tech hubs that attract startups and established businesses alike.
